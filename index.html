Selected work

Projects that left the lab

Speech systems running on real customer calls, and vision systems built to run in the field.

Telephone ASR that works on real calls

Production

Whisper and Parakeet fine-tuned on German telephone audio: word error rate on real customer calls from 39–52% (stock models) to 20%, and false transcriptions on pure background noise from 100% of clips to under 1%. Extended to English and Air Traffic Control audio.

Whisper on TensorRT + Triton

Production

Whisper and Parakeet converted to ONNX and TensorRT FP16 and served on NVIDIA Triton: Whisper 5x faster than PyTorch (658 → 127 ms per 10 s clip) and 1.9x faster than the JAX/Flax backend.

LLM reasoning for a voice agent

Production

A Qwen-class LLM on SGLang with speculative decoding as the reasoning module of a conversational-AI product: tool-call response from ~4 s to ~0.8 s, 2–3x faster than vLLM on long context.

Streaming TTS with voice cloning

Production

Real-time TTS with voice cloning from short reference audio, per-style LoRA and cross-call speaker consistency (Qwen3-TTS, IndexTTS-2). Found and fixed three bugs in vLLM-Omni serving for Qwen3-TTS.

Pistachio & nuts optical sorter

Industrial inspection systems
